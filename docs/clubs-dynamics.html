Clubs with the highest goals per appearance

Attack vs. defense

The club efficiency quadrant

Each bubble is a club, sized by recorded appearances. The vertical and horizontal references are the dataset’s appearance-weighted league means.

Lower on the vertical axis means fewer goals conceded; farther right means more goals scored. Hover labels carry exact club-level values.

Home-field advantage

Result shares and home scoring edge

Season-level shares compare home wins, draws and away wins. Hover also reports the home-minus-away goals-per-match edge and season sample size.

Home advantage is summarized from match outcomes and scores; this CSV does not measure travel, attendance or other explanatory factors.

All figures are computed from the checked-in match-level CSV. Paired xG is available only where both values are present; missing xG is never imputed. Descriptive patterns are not causal estimates.
